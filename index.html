  • Existing stores Fulfillment stays in the store. No dark-store network required.

Why grocery

Online is where grocery growth is happening, and picking those orders by hand is slow and labor-intensive. Most retailers face the same tradeoff: serve digital demand, or protect store margins.

“Manual store-based fulfilment tends to hit a ceiling at around 12-15% online penetration.” McKinsey & Company, The State of Grocery North America 2026
